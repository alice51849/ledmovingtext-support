1. What it is

LED Moving Text turns your iPhone or iPad into a big scrolling sign. Type a message and hold up your phone: from the back of a crowd, across an arrivals hall or over a busy counter, people read it in one glance.

  • It opens straight onto a live canvas: the preview is the editor, so words, speed, size and color change the moment you do.
  • Full screen with one tap; swipe for speed and direction, pinch to resize, turn the letters sideways to use the full height.
  • Cue Flow puts one message on each line and steps through them live or on a 2–20 second timer.
  • Mirrored text for a window or windscreen, adjustable blink rate and a flashlight pulse.
  • Ready-made boards written for each of fifty languages instead of translated word for word.
  • Works on iPhone and iPad in portrait, landscape and Split View.

This page follows the iOS App Store listing. An Android version is available on Google Play.

2. Who it is for

  • Concert and fan-event goers who want to cheer for a song without shouting themselves hoarse.
  • People meeting someone at arrivals who want to be spotted first.
  • Drivers and staff holding up a pickup message at the kerb or a counter.
  • Anyone who needs to ask for a quieter table or a slower sentence where speaking is hard, or leave a short notice on a desk.

3. How pricing works

LED Moving Text is a free download and the free part stays free, with nothing to sign up for: unlimited messages and full-screen display, still and scrolling motion, all 108 text colors, mirrored text, a sideways mode, blink rate, recent messages, one ready-made board, one LED frame and one saved board of your own. An optional one-time unlock adds unlimited saved boards, over 100 typefaces, 20 animation effects, the other 59 ready-made boards, more motions, rainbow and gradient lettering, photo and aurora backgrounds, beat-reactive motion, a countdown, and MP4 and GIF export without the watermark. The unlock is a one-time purchase; check the App Store page for the current price in your country.

Frequently asked questions

Is LED Moving Text free?

Yes. It is a free download, and unlimited messages and full-screen display are free forever with no sign-up.

What does the one-time unlock add?

Unlimited saved boards, over 100 typefaces, 20 animation effects, 59 more ready-made boards, extra motions, rainbow and gradient lettering, photo and aurora backgrounds, beat-reactive motion, a countdown and watermark-free MP4/GIF export.

Does it show ads?

No. There is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Can I show several messages in a row?

Yes. Cue Flow steps through one message per line, live or on a 2–20 second timer with optional repeat.

Does it use the microphone?

Only while beat-reactive motion is switched on, and audio is never recorded or saved.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes, on iPhone and iPad, with a full-width layout in portrait, landscape and Split View.
