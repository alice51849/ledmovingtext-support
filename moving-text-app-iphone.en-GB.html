Make moving text in four steps

  1. Type your message. The app opens straight onto a live canvas; the preview is the editor, so the text moves as you type.
  2. Pick a colour. All 108 text colours are included. Light text on a dark background reads best from a distance.
  3. Tap full screen. Tap again to show or hide the controls; the pause button stops the motion.
  4. Turn it sideways if you need bigger letters. The ninety-degree mode uses the full height of the screen for each letter.

Frequently asked questions

What is a moving text app?

It turns your phone screen into a scrolling sign. You type a message and it travels across the screen in large letters, so people can read it from a distance.

How do I make text move across my iPhone screen?

Open LED Moving Text, type your message and tap full screen. Swipe on the board to change the speed and direction, and pinch to resize the letters.

Can I stop the text from moving?

Yes. Choose still motion, or tap the pause button in full screen and the words stand still.

Can I save my moving text as a video?

The one-time unlock includes MP4 and GIF export without the watermark.

Does it need an account or an internet connection?

No. There is no account and everything runs on the device, so your messages stay on your iPhone.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes. It is built for iPhone and iPad, with layouts for portrait, landscape and Split View.
