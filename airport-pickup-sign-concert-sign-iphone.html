Steps: from blank screen to a readable sign in under a minute

  1. Type the message. LED Moving Text opens straight onto a live canvas: the preview is the editor, so you see the result as you type.
  2. Pick a color with contrast. All 108 text colors are included. Yellow, white or light green on black reads well in a dark hall; bright colors help in a busy terminal.
  3. Go full screen. One tap. Tap again to show or hide the controls, and use the pause button when you want the words to stand still.
  4. Turn it sideways. The ninety-degree mode uses the full height of the screen, so each letter is as tall as possible.
  5. Set the speed. Swipe left or right on the board to change speed and direction; pinch to resize the text.

Wording you can copy

Airport pickup sign: “WELCOME HOME, MAYA”, “SMITH FAMILY”, “Pickup: J. Chen”, “Door 4 → here”. Keep it to the name your guest will look for; a surname reads faster than a full sentence.

Concert sign: “PLAY OUR SONG”, “FIRST SHOW!”, “FROM TEXAS ♥”, “WAVE PLEASE”. Short words in big letters are easier to read from the stage than a long line.

Quiet requests: the listing also describes using a sign where speaking is hard, for example asking for a quieter table or a slower sentence, or leaving a short notice on a desk.

Cue Flow: a prepared run of messages

Some moments need more than one sign. With Cue Flow you put one message on each line, then step backward or forward live, or choose a timer between 2 and 20 seconds with optional repeat. Event crews, speakers and queues get a prepared run of messages without leaving the full-screen board.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use my iPhone as an airport pickup sign?

Yes. Type the name, go full screen and turn the letters sideways so they fill the screen height. Keep the message short so it can be read from across the arrivals hall.

How do I make a concert sign on my phone?

Type two to five words, choose a bright color on a dark background, set a slow scroll and hold the phone up in landscape. Check the venue rules first.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Everything runs on the device, so the sign works in a basement venue or a crowded terminal.

Can I show the text on a car window?

Yes. Mirrored text makes the words read correctly from outside the glass.

Do I need an account?

No account and nothing to sign up for. Messages and saved boards stay on your iPhone.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes. It is built for iPhone and iPad, with layouts for portrait, landscape and Split View.
