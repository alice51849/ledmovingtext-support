What it is

LED Moving Text turns an iPhone or iPad into a bright scrolling sign. Type a message, hold up the phone, and people can read it at a glance from the back of a crowd or across an arrivals hall.

  • It opens straight onto a live canvas: the preview is the editor, so words, speed, size and color change as you edit.
  • Full screen with tap to show or hide controls, a clearly labeled pause button, swipe for speed and direction, pinch to resize, and a sideways ninety-degree mode.
  • Cue Flow puts one message on each line and steps through them live or on a 2–20 second timer, with optional repeat.
  • Mirrored text for a window or windscreen, 108 text colors, adjustable blink rate and a flashlight pulse.

Who it is for

  • Concert and sports fans who want to cheer without shouting themselves hoarse.
  • Anyone meeting someone at airport arrivals or holding up a pickup message at the kerb.
  • People in loud or quiet places where speaking is hard, for example asking for a quieter table.
  • Anyone who wants to leave a short notice on a desk while stepping away.

How it is priced

It costs nothing to download and there is nothing to sign up for. Unlimited messages and full-screen display, still and scrolling motion, speed and size, all 108 colors, mirrored text, the sideways mode, recent messages, one ready-made board, one LED frame and one saved board are included at no charge, permanently. A one-time in-app unlock adds unlimited saved boards, over one hundred typefaces, twenty animation effects, the other fifty-nine ready-made boards, more motion styles, photo and aurora backgrounds, beat-reactive motion, a countdown and MP4 and GIF export without a watermark. There is no subscription.

What sets it apart

  • The preview is the editor, so there is no separate settings screen to dig through.
  • Cue Flow steps through several messages without leaving full screen.
  • Fifty languages, with the ready-made boards written for each language instead of translated word for word.
  • No account, no cloud, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The microphone is used only while beat-reactive motion is on, and audio is never recorded or saved.
  • Layouts for portrait, landscape and Split View on iPhone and iPad.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use my iPhone as a concert or airport pickup sign?

Yes. Type a name or message, go full screen and hold up the phone. You can change speed and size, turn the letters sideways for more height and pause the motion with one button.

Can I show several messages in a row?

Yes. Cue Flow puts one message on each line and steps through them live or on a 2 to 20 second timer, with optional repeat, without leaving full screen.

Do I need an account or a subscription?

No account and no subscription. The core sign features are included at no charge, and a one-time in-app unlock adds more typefaces, effects, boards and export.

Does it show ads or collect my messages?

No. There is no advertising, analytics or tracking, and your messages, boards and photos stay on your iPhone.

Does it use the microphone?

Only while beat-reactive motion is switched on, and audio is never recorded or saved.
